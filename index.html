About the System

The Campus Maintenance Fault Reporting System allows students to report maintenance problems through a simple online form. Submitted reports are stored in a database where they can be reviewed by the maintenance department.

Fault Categories

Electrical

Broken lights, sockets and electrical faults.

Plumbing

Leaking taps, blocked drains and water issues.

Furniture

Damaged desks, chairs and classroom furniture.

Internet

Wi-Fi and campus network connectivity problems.
